# Prestige design benchmarks

Research conducted on 12 October 2026 using official website content retrieved through Agent Reach's web reader. Tom Hartley's linked stylesheet was also inspected. These observations cover source content, structure and declared styling; competitor screenshots and computed browser styling were unavailable.

| Benchmark | Observed pattern | Impulse adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Tom Hartley](https://www.tomhartley.com/) | Media-led hero with one inventory action. Official CSS uses serif headings, monochrome sections, generous vertical spacing and square buttons. | A cinematic static hero, Impulse's own tagline, one stock action and a quieter showroom link. |
| [Romans International](https://www.romansinternational.com/) | Latest arrivals show photographs, price, year and mileage. Stock, selling and dealership history have separate entry points. | Clear vehicle facts, a photograph-led homepage collection and a distinct story section. |
| [Rolls-Royce Motor Cars](https://www.rolls-roycemotorcars.com/) | Short media-backed campaign headings, sparse discovery actions and thematic exploration cards. | Shorter copy, fewer competing controls and deliberate section transitions. |
| [Bentley Motors](https://www.bentleymotors.com/) | Large campaign imagery, concise model propositions and clear test-drive/dealer routes. | Consistent image treatment, restrained display typography and easy enquiry/showroom access. |
| [Lorbek Luxury Cars](https://www.lorbek.com.au/home) | Photo-led arrivals include price, mileage, transmission and government-charge disclosures; showroom and customer information support trust. | Readable stock facts, genuine supplied customer quotes and the verified Elizabeth South contact details. |

The resulting design is original to Impulse. Its monochrome wordmark and car/handshake/pulse motif follow the user's Facebook screenshots. Competitor logos, photographs, proprietary fonts and CSS have not been copied.

The example catalogue now matches the makes and model families visible in the supplied template photographs. Years, prices, kilometres and specifications remain illustrative. Galleries show only relevant available model photographs; a single image has no redundant previous/next controls. Actual Impulse stock and original showroom photographs should replace the example content for a live dealership site.

Mobile spacing, readable vehicle facts, keyboard focus, Escape handling and reduced-motion preferences are part of the visual standard, alongside stock filters, saved cars, galleries and enquiry preparation.
